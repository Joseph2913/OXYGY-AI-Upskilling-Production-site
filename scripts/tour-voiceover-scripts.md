## Welcome / Opt-in (tour-welcome.mp3)

Welcome to the Oxygy AI Upskilling platform. Over the next few moments, I'll walk you through each section of the app so you know exactly where everything lives and how to get the most out of your learning experience. Let's get started.

---

## Step 1 — My Journey (tour-step-1.mp3)

This is your Journey page — your personalised learning roadmap. You'll see up to five levels laid out here, each one building on the last. Every level has three things you need to complete: an interactive e-learning module, a hands-on toolkit activity where you'll use one of our AI tools, and a project submission where you apply what you've learned to a real task from your own work. You can track your progress across all three from right here.

---

## Step 2 — Dashboard (tour-step-2.mp3)

This is your Dashboard — think of it as your control centre. At a glance, you can see your overall completion, your current learning streak, and where you left off. If you're part of a team cohort, you'll also see a leaderboard showing how your colleagues are progressing. It's a great way to stay motivated and pick up exactly where you left off each time you log in.

---

## Step 3 — Current Level (tour-step-3.mp3)

Here's your learning environment. This is where the e-learning modules live — fully interactive lessons with slides, activities, and quizzes. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can close the browser and come back exactly where you left off. For the best experience, we recommend enabling the narration feature — it adds a guided audio layer to every slide. You can also go fullscreen to remove distractions and focus entirely on the content.

---

## Step 4 — My Toolkit (tour-step-4.mp3)

Welcome to your Toolkit. This is where you'll find the AI-powered tools that go hand in hand with each level. There are five tools in total, one for each level, and they unlock as you complete the e-learning. Each tool follows the same structured flow: you describe your task, the AI generates a tailored output, and you can save the result to your Artefacts. Let's take a closer look at the first one.

---

## Step 5 — Prompt Playground (tour-step-5.mp3)

This is the Prompt Playground — your Level 1 toolkit tool. You start by describing a task you want to accomplish, and the AI generates a well-structured prompt using proven strategies like role framing, structured output, and constraint framing. You can see exactly which strategies were applied and why, refine the prompt further, and then save your best work to the Prompt Library for easy reuse. It's a great way to build up a collection of high-quality prompts over time.

---

## Step 6 — Learning Coach (tour-step-6.mp3)

This is your Learning Coach. It curates external learning resources that are tailored to whatever topic you're currently studying. You choose a platform — like YouTube, Perplexity, or NotebookLM — set your learning objective, and the Coach generates a personalised study plan with handpicked resources. It's an optional but powerful complement to the core e-learning modules.

---

## Step 7 — My Artefacts (tour-step-7.mp3)

Here's your Artefacts page. Every time you create something with a toolkit tool — a prompt, an agent, a workflow, a dashboard — it gets saved here automatically. Think of it as your portfolio of AI work. You can search, filter, and open any artefact to pick up where you left off. The arrow icon on each one takes you straight back to the tool page where it was created.

---

## Step 8 — My Cohort (tour-step-8.mp3)

Finally, this is your Cohort page. If you're enrolled in a team cohort, this is where you see how everyone's progressing together — a leaderboard, weekly activity patterns, and learning milestones. It's designed to create a sense of shared momentum as your team moves through the programme. And that's the full tour — you're all set to start learning. Good luck!
